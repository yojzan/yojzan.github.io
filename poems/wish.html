Untamed

The wish

One candle, the wax going soft
in a room I am not standing in.

I am a voice in a phone face-down on a table,
and you are somewhere in this same city,
counting the same hours.

So here is the wish, stated plainly:
that the year ahead has fewer locked doors,
that the people who make you wait
learn to say your name without flinching,
that you stop apologizing for a distance
that was never yours to carry.

And the greedier wish, the one I don't say aloud:
two cups on one table,
a morning with no one checking who is watching,
the ordinary small things,
unguarded.

Until then I give you words,
which are not enough,
which are all my hands can hold tonight.

Blow it out. Mean it.
