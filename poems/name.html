Untamed

The Way Your Name Returns

When your name finds mine,
I wonder whether I am loving you,
or merely returning
to the place where love first found me,
to have that scent pass over me again.

No blessing has ever left me
feeling as forgiven
as the simple way
you say my name.

The old temples never kept me.
The stone remembered every prayer,
yet your silence has always known
exactly where to find mine.

Even my longing betrays me.
I walked toward you like an ocean,
my arms open with everything to give,
only to discover
the rivers had been moving toward me all along.

I find myself standing again,
inside the impossible certainty
that it had always been you
only smiling,

for me to feel love.
