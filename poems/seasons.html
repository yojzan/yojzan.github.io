Untamed

A Season It Has Never Seen

The calendar keeps its own rules.
Every month I count the days
like a man counting stones across a river
he is not yet allowed to cross.

Here, the city hums its ordinary hum.
Buses, shutters, someone's radio.
You are inside that hum somewhere,
a single voice I can almost place.

Our words travel the short way, through screens,
and arrive smaller than we send them.
Still I keep sending.
Still you answer, which is its own kind of arriving.

Today the world turns a little for you.
Let it turn slowly.
When the door opens on its own,
may you walk through it without looking back
at who was watching.

Until that day, I am here,
writing the same hope in a new hand,
waiting the way a root waits
for a season it has never seen.
