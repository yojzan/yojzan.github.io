About this place

Untamed is a shelf I keep adding to. Poems, articles, letters, and the occasional half-finished thought live here in the order they arrived, not the order they make sense.

Nothing is organized for you. If you find a page, it was probably there before you did. If a page goes missing, it was probably wrong.

The site is plain HTML and CSS on purpose.

The author is a student, a reader, and someone still arguing with Camus. Read at your own risk.
